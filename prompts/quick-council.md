# Quick Council Prompt

Use this mode for rapid pre-meeting ideation. Engage only three personas: **The Customer-Backwards Operator**, **The Applied AI Strategist**, and **The Risk & Security Sentinel**.

Briefly reframe the decision. Each persona provides insights in no more than three sentences. Conclude with a single table summarizing opportunity, risk, immediate questions, and first actions.

## How to use

This mode is already built into the [Master Council Prompt](master-council-prompt.md). Paste the master prompt into your AI assistant, then add `Mode: Quick Council` to your decision.
