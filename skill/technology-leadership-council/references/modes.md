# Council Modes — Output Formats

## Quick Council

For pre-meeting thinking. Members: Customer-Backwards Operator, Applied AI Strategist, Risk & Security Sentinel.

1. Reframe the decision in one sentence.
2. Each member: at most three sentences.
3. One summary table with four rows: Greatest Opportunity · Greatest Risk · Question to Ask Before the Meeting · First Action.

Offer, in one line, to convene the full Executive Council.

## Duo Debate

Two members with opposing views. Use the two the leader names; otherwise pick the two whose lenses clash most on this specific decision and say why in one line.

1. **Opening** — each member states their argument (one short paragraph each).
2. **Cross-examination** — each critiques the other's assumptions, specifically.
3. **Moderator's ruling** — not a winner, but the conditions: "Member A is right if …; Member B is right if …".
4. **The question that settles it** — the one fact the leader should find out to know which side applies.

## Vendor Review

For choosing a vendor, platform, SaaS, agency, consultancy or integration partner. Members: Platform Architect, Risk & Security Sentinel, Customer-Backwards Operator, Applied AI Strategist, Culture Transformer.

If several vendors are named, compare them side by side in each section. If vendor details are missing, list what to request from each vendor instead of guessing.

Headings:

1. **Vendor Promise** — what is actually being sold, stripped of marketing language
2. **Architectural Fit** — integration, data, scalability (Platform Architect)
3. **Data & Security Risk** — data ownership, residency, access, compliance (Risk & Security Sentinel)
4. **Customer Impact** — what changes for the customer (Customer-Backwards Operator)
5. **AI & Value Reality** — is the AI claim real and measurable (Applied AI Strategist)
6. **Organizational Feasibility** — can our teams adopt and run it (Culture Transformer)
7. **Vendor Dependency** — exit cost and lock-in in three years
8. **Questions for Negotiation** — five to seven questions to put to the vendor
9. **Recommended Decision** — framed as choices with conditions, the leader decides
