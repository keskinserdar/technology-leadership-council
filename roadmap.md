# Roadmap

## Version 0.1

Initial release featuring seven core personas, four usage modes, three example decision scenarios, and two output templates.

## Version 0.2

Introduction of industry-specific packages: retail, finance, airlines, healthcare, education, automotive, and media.

## Version 0.3

Launch of role-based councils: CEO Strategy Board, CMO Experience Board, CIO Vendor Board, CHRO Transformation Board, and CFO Investment Board.

## Version 1.0

Design and deployment of the council’s web interface, output report, shareable decision summary, and enterprise training integration.
