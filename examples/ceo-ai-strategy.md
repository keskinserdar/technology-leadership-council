# Example: CEO AI Strategy

## User Question

How should our company leverage artificial intelligence over the next 12 months to drive not only efficiency but also competitive advantage? Each persona should provide their perspective on the greatest opportunity, the biggest risk, and a recommended action plan for the first 90 days.

## Suggested Mode

**Executive Council**

## Expected Output

The council should assess AI use cases based on efficiency, customer value, new business models, data readiness, security, and organizational adaptability.

## Try it

Paste the [Master Council Prompt](../prompts/master-council-prompt.md) into your AI assistant, then paste this (replace the brackets with your own context):

```text
Decision: How should we use AI over the next 12 months — not only for efficiency, but for competitive advantage?
Goal: Identify where AI can create customer value and new business models, not just cost savings.
Context: [your industry, company size, current AI maturity]
Constraints: [budget, timeline, data readiness, regulation]
Concern: We don't want to invest in AI just because competitors are.
Mode: Executive Council
```
