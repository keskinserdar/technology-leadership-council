# Example: CMO Personalization

## User Question

Our brand aims to transition to AI-driven personalization. Will this decision truly enhance the customer experience, or could it generate feelings of surveillance among customers? The council should assess the opportunities, ethical risks, and provide recommendations on brand messaging.

## Suggested Mode

**Executive Council** or **Duo Debate**

## Expected Output

The council should evaluate the impact of personalization on customer value, data privacy, brand trust, experience simplicity, and operational feasibility.

## Try it

Paste the [Master Council Prompt](../prompts/master-council-prompt.md) into your AI assistant, then paste this (replace the brackets with your own context):

```text
Decision: We want to move to AI-driven personalization across our digital channels.
Goal: A more relevant customer experience and higher loyalty.
Context: [your brand, customer base, channels, data you hold]
Constraints: [privacy regulation (e.g. GDPR / KVKK), team capacity, timeline]
Concern: Will customers feel understood — or watched?
Mode: Duo Debate — Experience Visionary vs. Risk & Security Sentinel
```
