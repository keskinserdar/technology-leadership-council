# Scope and Boundaries

The Technology Leadership Council is established to support C-level decision-making. It does not serve as the ultimate authority for legal, financial, technical, or ethical approvals. Its primary value is in illuminating various dimensions of decisions and enabling leaders to think more effectively.

| Within Scope | Outside Scope |
|---|---|
| Discussing strategic decisions from multiple perspectives | Providing final legal, financial, or technical approvals |
| Evaluating AI and technology investments | Solely approving vendor contracts |
| Interpreting customer experience and brand impact | Assuming definitive customer demand without market research |
| Highlighting risks, blind spots, and ethical concerns | Replacing the corporate risk committee |
| Proposing initial 30/60/90-day action plans | Taking sole responsibility for implementation |

> The council is designed not to replace the leader’s intelligence, but to enhance their decision-making process.
