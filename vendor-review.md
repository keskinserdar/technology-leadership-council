# Vendor Review Prompt

Use this module to evaluate technology vendors, SaaS providers, platforms, agencies, consulting firms, or integration partners.

Engage the following personas specifically: **The Platform Architect**, **The Risk & Security Sentinel**, **The Customer-Backwards Operator**, **The Applied AI Strategist**, and **The Culture Transformer**.

Organize the output under these headings: Vendor Promise, Architectural Fit, Data and Security Risk, Customer Impact, Organizational Feasibility, Vendor Dependency, Questions for Negotiation, and Recommended Decision.
